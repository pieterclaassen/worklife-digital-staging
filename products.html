Products & diagnostics

Measure what keeps people—and organisations—healthy.

WorkLife Digital combines psychological science, organisational diagnostics and practical development tools. Some offerings are WorkLife products; others use established third-party assessment instruments delivered by qualified practitioners.

WorkLife product

People Sustainability Quotient (PSQ)

Our flagship people-sustainability diagnostic. PSQ examines the psychological conditions that help people remain healthy, adaptive and effective, together with organisational conditions that support sustainable performance.

PSQ is the next evolution of the earlier WorkLife / Wellbeing Quotient work.

Assessment

Leadership assessment & development

Evidence-based leadership assessment, structured feedback, individual development planning and coaching for leaders and leadership teams.

Third-party assessment

Hogan assessments

Personality and leadership assessment using the Hogan Assessment Suite, with interpretation and development feedback by a qualified organisational psychologist.

Organisation

Culture & organisational diagnostics

Culture audits, organisational-health diagnostics, team dynamics and evidence-led organisational development. This includes work supporting merger integration and large-scale organisational change.

WorkLife offering

Stay-at-Work

A diagnostic approach to retention and employee experience, combining psychological insight with organisational evidence to understand why people stay, disengage or leave.

Workshop / intervention

HumanWorksX

Facilitated work focused on leadership, culture and healthier organisational systems—turning psychological insight into practical organisational action.

Digital integration

Psychology API & digital assessments

Evidence-based diagnostics designed for digital delivery and integration into HR, coaching and enterprise platforms.
