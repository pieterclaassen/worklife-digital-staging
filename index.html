Sustainable performance

People. Planet. Profit.

Organisations don't operate in isolation. Sustainable performance depends on understanding the relationship between people, organisational performance and the wider systems in which organisations operate.

01

People

People need the psychological, relational and organisational conditions to perform well — without performance coming at an unsustainable human cost.

02

Planet

Organisations are part of wider social and ecological systems. What happens inside an organisation cannot be entirely separated from its impact on the world around it.

03

Profit

Organisations need to perform. Sustainable organisations convert human capability, sound decisions and organisational effectiveness into enduring commercial and operational results.
