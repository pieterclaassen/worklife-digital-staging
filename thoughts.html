A small selection of Anna-Rosa's writing that connects psychological health, organisational resilience and sustainable performance.

Organisational wellbeing · 2019

The Enneagram and Organisational Wellness

This article makes the clearest case for the WorkLife thesis: stress, burnout and poor psychological health are not only individual problems—they affect productivity, engagement, absenteeism and an organisation's ability to thrive. It also shows how psychological data can be used to identify risk and guide targeted interventions.

Organisational change · 2019

Using the Enneagram to Make Your Organisation Future-fit

Future-ready organisations depend on people who can adapt, understand their own behaviour and work effectively through change. Anna-Rosa explores how deeper insight into human motivation can support resilient teams, leadership development and organisational transformation.

Performance & leadership · 2019

Performance Management and the Enneagram in the New World of Work

This piece argues that performance is strengthened through trust, regular feedback, coaching and better leadership conversations rather than relying only on traditional performance-management systems. It reinforces the idea that organisational performance is inseparable from the quality of the human system behind it.
